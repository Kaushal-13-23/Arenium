ABOUT THE FEST

The Arena of Innovation · Annual National Inter-College Tech Fest

ARENIUM'27 is the annual national technology festival hosted by the School of Information Science at Presidency University, Bengaluru.

Taking place on 4–5 February 2027 at the Presidency University campus, the festival welcomes undergraduate and postgraduate students from institutions nationwide to compete across 10 technical and cultural events with a total cash prize pool of ₹1,50,000.

Featured events include Tech Odyssey, ShadowCode, DesignNova, TechTalk, DataChronicles, Founders' Arena, Technova '27 (24-Hour Hackathon), Quad Damage (Esports), Biochroma, and APL (Arenium Premier League).
